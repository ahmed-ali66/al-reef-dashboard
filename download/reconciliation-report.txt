   an outstanding balance that was NOT carried forward, this creates an inflation.
   Additionally, the old bug where totalAmountDue was overwritten to currentOutstanding
   after payments also contributed to data corruption.

   Total understatement: 8624.49 AED

   cycle outstandingAmount was NOT always updated correctly, or payments were allocated
   to the bill but not to a specific cycle, leaving the cycle outstanding higher.

3. SAFETY ASSESSMENT:

     b) The old payment bug where totalAmountDue was set to currentOutstanding
     c) Manual edits via PUT /api/recurring-bills/:id that could set arbitrary values
